Verb Deep-Dive: tener (to have)

Possession, plus the everyday way to say "have to," plus a whole family of idioms.
tener is a full verb — no modal mechanics needed. It means straightforward possession, but its two extra jobs (tener que + infinitive for obligation, and tener + noun idioms) make it do double duty across the language.
