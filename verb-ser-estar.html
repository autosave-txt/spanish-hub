Verb Deep-Dive: ser & estar (to be)

Spanish's biggest structural fork — two "to be" verbs that are NOT interchangeable.
English and German both get by with one "to be" verb. Spanish splits the job in two: ser is for lasting identity — what something IS at its core (origin, profession, physical traits, time, relationships). estar is for temporary state or location — how something IS right now, or where it is. Picking the wrong one is one of the most common mistakes English speakers make in Spanish, and it can genuinely change the meaning of a sentence, not just the grammar.
